About MEL ONE

Founded in 2011. Adelaide carpentry, joinery and maintenance for homeowners and real estate agents — built on clear communication, practical support and fair pricing.

Our Story & Values

MEL ONE was founded in 2011 and has spent the years since building long-term partnerships with homeowners, real estate agents, builders and property managers across Adelaide and South Australia. Through clear communication, practical support and fair pricing, we have grown almost entirely on repeat business and word-of-mouth referrals — the kind of trust that only comes from showing up, quoting honestly and standing behind every job.

R

Real-World Experience

Our tradespeople bring years of on-the-tools experience across structural carpentry, fine joinery, heritage repair and routine maintenance. We use the right equipment for Australian homes and understand the local conditions that affect timber work — from coastal salt air to Adelaide Hills climate to termite-prone suburbs.

  • 13+ years working across Adelaide homes and businesses
  • Equipment suited to on-site structural work and in-shop joinery
  • Working knowledge of SA building codes and NCC requirements
  • Experienced across heritage repair, modern construction and custom fit-out
I

Integrity First

We believe home maintenance and timber work should come with honest advice, reliable service and proper follow-up. Every quote is itemised, every schedule is realistic, and every job is finished to a standard we are willing to put our name to.

  • Itemised quotes — no hidden costs or surprise variations
  • Realistic scheduling — we arrive when we say we will
  • Worksite left clean and tidy at the end of each day
  • Workmanship backed by a written guarantee
L

Long-Term Partnerships

Most of our work comes from clients who call us back and refer us on. That only happens when you keep your promises on price, quality and timing. We are building the kind of Adelaide business people recommend to their neighbours — one job at a time.

  • Most work is repeat business or direct referral
  • You deal with the tradesperson doing the work — no call centre
  • Consistent team from quote through to completion
  • Property managers and agents welcome ongoing service arrangements

What We Deliver

MEL ONE is an Adelaide carpentry and joinery company covering on-site structural work, in-house fine joinery and cabinetry, and restoration and maintenance — from the building frame to the finished piece, all delivered by one team under one standard of quality.
