# Footer social links

The shared footer in build.mjs adds FOLLOW MEL ONE after the existing footer columns on every generated page. Visible names supply accessible link names; logos have empty alt text and explicit 24 × 24 dimensions. Links open a new tab with noopener noreferrer. The list wraps horizontally and links have a minimum 44px height.

All four images are self-hosted in src/assets and copied by the build allowlist.

Downloaded unchanged on 2026-10-10:

- Google multicolor G: https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/googleg/1x/googleg_standard_color_64dp.png (official Google-hosted asset; not CC0).
- Instagram: https://cdn.simpleicons.org/instagram/E4405F
- YouTube: https://cdn.simpleicons.org/youtube/FF0000
- TikTok: https://cdn.simpleicons.org/tiktok/FFFFFF

The three SVGs are supplied by Simple Icons, not downloaded from the platforms themselves.
