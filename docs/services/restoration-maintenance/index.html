Restoration & Maintenance

Older Adelaide homes carry real termite risk. We offer assessment-to-repair in one team and advise on prevention.

Termite damage repair

With a licensed pest controller, we assess extent and impact, replace damaged frames, floor joists and door jambs, and install termite barriers.

Moisture rot repair

Repair of water-damaged timber with waterproofing, ventilation improvement, member replacement and damp-proof coating.

Structural assessment

Pre-repair structural safety check, with engineer's report where needed to meet the Building Code.
