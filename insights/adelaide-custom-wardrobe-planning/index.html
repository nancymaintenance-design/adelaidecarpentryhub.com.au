Adelaide Custom Wardrobe Planning: How to Measure, Specify and Avoid Storage Dead Space

A wardrobe works best when its internal layout follows the way a household stores clothing, linen and everyday items—not just the dimensions of an empty wall.

Measure the room, then measure the obstructions

Overall width, height and depth are only the first layer. Note skirtings, cornices, power points, windows, light switches, return walls and door swings, plus any variation in floor or wall level. In older Adelaide homes, walls and ceilings are not always square; an on-site measure before manufacture is the point at which the cabinet dimensions, filler panels and scribe details can be finalised.

Turn daily habits into internal zones

List what the wardrobe must hold before selecting finishes. Long-hang clothing, folded garments, shoes, linen, bags, drawers and seasonal storage each need different clearances. A useful layout normally mixes adjustable shelves with dedicated hanging zones and avoids deep, unlit shelves that become dead space. Consider who uses each side of the wardrobe and whether future changes—such as a child growing or a room changing function—are likely.

Select doors and materials for the room, not the showroom

Hinged doors need clear swing space; sliding doors preserve circulation but limit access to part of the interior at once. Mirrored, painted and timber-look finishes each change light, maintenance and the visual weight of the room. For wardrobes near bathrooms, laundries or external walls, discuss moisture exposure and ventilation so the substrate, edging and installation detail suit the location.

Plan the installation as carefully as the cabinet

Confirm access from the street to the room, stair turns, parking, flooring protection and the timing of painting, electrical work and new carpet. A clear installation sequence reduces the risk that joinery is measured before adjoining finishes are ready. When requesting a quote, provide photos, a rough sketch, preferred finishes and a list of the contents the wardrobe needs to store.
