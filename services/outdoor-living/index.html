Outdoor Living

Adelaide's coastal climate is hard on outdoor timber. We specify certified preservative-treated timber with proper drainage and fixing so your outdoor space stays looking new for years.

Timber decking

Merbau, Spotted Gum or Treated Pine decks with vented sub-structure, drainage gaps and slip-resistant finish for homes, pools and commercial areas.

Pergolas & shade structures

Freestanding or attached pergolas with weatherproof joints, steel connectors and treated hardwood, ready for motorised shade or greenery.

Fencing

Termite-resistant treated timber fences, custom oiling and staining, with concrete footings and wind bracing for boundary and privacy needs.

Coastal preservative timber

H4/H5 treated timber with stainless fixings and a maintenance plan for Adelaide's salt-air coastal suburbs.
