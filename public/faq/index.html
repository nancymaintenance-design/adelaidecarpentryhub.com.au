What happens before I receive a quote?

Start with the timber item, intended change and Adelaide location. For several small repairs, list each item so the visit and materials can be scoped together. We arrange a site assessment or measure and confirm any charges before booking. Compare quotes for the same quantities, materials, labour, supply or collection, access, removal, waste, finishing and GST treatment. Ask whether the price is fixed or an estimate, what is excluded, and how concealed damage or changed selections are priced and authorised. Confirm payment arrangements, quote validity and whether design work or further investigation is needed. An hourly rate alone does not show the total project cost.

Does peeling fascia paint mean the whole board needs replacing?

Peeling paint alone does not establish the repair extent. MEL ONE checks timber condition, board ends, joints, fixings and the adjoining base before comparing local repair, section replacement and full-board replacement. Painting and timber replacement are specified separately, so the quote explains both the repair and the intended finish.

  • Fascia, Bargeboard & Timber Eaves Repairs
Can one damaged weatherboard be replaced?

Yes, MEL ONE provides single-board and multiple-board local replacement. The workable repair extent depends on adjoining timber, fixing positions, board profile and nearby trims. An onsite assessment establishes whether an individual board repair is suitable or whether a local group needs replacement to form the agreed junctions.

  • Local Weatherboard Repairs
Does a squeaky or bouncing floor mean the joists need replacing?

No single symptom establishes that conclusion. Floorboards, fixings, supporting members and connections can produce different movement or noise problems. MEL ONE inspects the safely accessible area to distinguish those possibilities and defines any further professional assessment before confirming local strengthening, replacement or a connection repair.

  • Subfloor Timber Repairs
Is lowering a timber threshold enough to improve an entrance?

It may be one part of the work, but the person's movement needs, entrance height, door swing, doorway width, landing and ground conditions must be considered together. MEL ONE assesses the proposed timber adjustment and adjoining junctions before confirming the scope. A formal accessibility outcome needs the relevant assessment and specified design rather than an assumption based on threshold height alone.

  • Accessibility Carpentry & Timber Ramps
Can just one rotten pergola post be replaced?

A single-post replacement may be suitable after the remaining timber, connections and supports are assessed. For a load-bearing member, confirm the design, temporary support, sequence and necessary approval arrangements before removal. A leaning structure should not be treated as a simple decorative replacement.

  • Timber Pergola Repairs
What is the difference between a stair tread and a stringer?

A tread is the walking surface; a stringer supports the steps along the stair. Damage to a stringer is not covered simply by replacing a tread and should be assessed as a structural concern.

Can damaged cabinet hinge holes be repaired?

Some local fixing areas can be repaired when sufficient suitable material remains. Swollen board or extensive damage around the hinge may require a separately scoped board repair or replacement; a new hinge alone may not resolve it.
