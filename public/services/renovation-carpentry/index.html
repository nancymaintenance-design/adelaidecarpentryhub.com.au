Renovation Carpentry

MEL ONE coordinates carpentry within Adelaide renovations, where existing fabric and new finishes have to meet. Define the rooms, work stages and responsible trades before opening-up or committing to final trim and joinery interfaces.

Renovation carpentry starts with the existing home

An existing home brings conditions that drawings may not show: uneven walls, changed floor levels, previous alterations, concealed services, old trim and materials that need careful matching. We begin by understanding the rooms, the proposed changes and what can be observed before work starts. This creates a practical carpentry scope for altered openings, repairs and finishes without assuming that an older room will behave like a new empty shell.

Defining the timber scope across a renovation

Renovation carpentry can include framing changes directed by approved documentation, door openings, jambs, skirtings, architraves, local timber repairs, joinery interfaces and finishing details. The best scope is clear about which elements belong to MEL ONE and which are managed by the builder, designer, plumber, electrician, plasterer, painter or cabinetmaker. Clear boundaries protect the project program and help the owner understand what needs to happen before each room can be finished.

Openings, trims and finished floor levels

Doorways, windows, cabinetry and floor transitions are where a renovation becomes visibly precise or visibly unresolved. The jamb depth, trim profile, reveal, skirting height and floor finish need to be considered together. We discuss those details before final fixing, particularly when new timber flooring, tile, carpet or cabinetry will change existing levels. This reduces the need for improvised packing, uneven lines or repeated trimming after other work is complete.

Rental property and property-manager carpentry enquiries

For a rental repair list, name the person authorised to approve the assessment, quote and any variations. Include the property location, affected timber items, tenant or building-manager access arrangements and any limits on working hours. We inspect the accessible condition and define the carpentry tasks rather than assuming every reported fault has the same cause. Tenancy arrangements and permissions must be resolved by the responsible parties before work is booked.

Separate agreed repairs from extra work

Keep the work order, approved items and any exclusions together. Ask what completion notes, photos or product information will be supplied for the agreed job; do not assume a specialist inspection report is included. If opening-up reveals more damage, document the finding and obtain approval for the revised scope, cost and timing. A pre-sale or end-of-lease brief does not guarantee a sale outcome, inspection result or bond return.

Local timber repair within broader work

Removing an old cabinet, changing a wall or opening a lining can reveal damaged trim, missing skirting, rough framing or water-affected timber. MEL ONE checks the accessible area on site, defines the initial carpentry scope and discusses any opening-up needed. We agree changes to the repair plan, price and timing with you before additional work proceeds.

Coordinating cabinetry and other trades

Kitchen, bathroom, storage and architectural joinery work depends on finished walls, floors, services and openings being ready at the right time. Carpentry may need to create backing, trim an opening, prepare a wall junction or complete skirtings after built-ins are fitted. Share the current drawings, finishes and trade program where available. Coordinating these interfaces early helps avoid cabinetry being measured before adjacent construction is settled or completed trim being damaged by later installation.

Staging work around everyday living

Many Adelaide renovations take place while the property remains occupied. Access, dust, temporary security, room-by-room sequencing, furniture protection and the availability of bathrooms or kitchens can all affect how carpentry is scheduled. Let us know which rooms must stay usable and the preferred work windows. A realistic staged plan helps the owner, builder and trades decide what can be completed together and what should wait until adjoining work is ready.

Preparing a renovation carpentry brief

Useful information includes current plans or sketches, photos of each affected room, the address, approximate project timing, intended finishes and a list of the outcomes you want. Mark altered openings, new doors, storage, kitchen or bathroom work, damaged timber and areas needing new trim. This lets MEL ONE discuss an Adelaide renovation carpentry scope that links the practical timber tasks to the wider project rather than treating each small junction as an isolated repair.

Plan the renovation sequence — questions

Can renovation carpentry be staged while we live at home?

Staging depends on safe separation, access, dust, services and the rooms affected. Agree usable areas and daily handover with the project team before the sequence is booked.

Does this service include permission to remove a wall?

No. Structural implications, design, approvals and services must be resolved with the responsible professionals before an affected wall or opening is altered.

What should a property manager include in a carpentry work order?

List the affected items, site location, authorising contact, tenant or building-manager access and any spending or timing limits. Confirm the assessment and written quote before booking. Agree the completion record and how unexpected damage or variations will be authorised rather than treating the original repair list as unlimited approval.
