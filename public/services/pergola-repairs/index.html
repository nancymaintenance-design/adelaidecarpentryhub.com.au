Timber Pergola Repairs

MEL ONE repairs existing timber pergolas in Adelaide, including local component replacement and structural repairs after assessment. A rotted post base, split beam end and loose connection need different repair scopes; we check the affected timber and supports before agreeing work.

Rotted pergola post bases and split beam ends

Post bases, beam ends and connections are useful starting points when timber is soft, cracked or moving. Record the affected member and whether movement is new rather than assuming the damage is cosmetic. A pergola that leans or has unstable connections should have access restricted until assessed. We inspect accessible timber, its support and adjacent components before deciding what can remain and what needs replacement.

Local repair or component replacement

The repair scope can include damaged posts, beams, upper timber members and decorative battens, along with failed connection hardware. Local repair is different from wrapping damaged wood with a new face board. The retained material and connections need to suit the intended repair. We identify the affected components and the work required around them, so a price for one member is not mistaken for a complete structure overhaul.

Structural replacement needs a planned sequence

A load-bearing post or beam cannot simply be removed like a loose decorative slat. Design requirements, temporary support, access and the replacement sequence must be established before the affected work proceeds. Any necessary approval arrangements and professional assessment are confirmed for the proposed repair. MEL ONE separates this structural scope from non-structural decorative timber repairs in the written quote.

Why ground-level moisture matters

A post base that stays wet can deteriorate differently from exposed upper timber. Drainage, contact with the ground, trapped water and end protection influence the repair detail. Beam ends and connections can also hold moisture out of sight. We consider the conditions around the damage and identify any separate drainage or adjoining work; replacing timber alone does not prove that the source of wetting has been resolved.

A leaning post is not just a painting job

For example, a soft post base with a leaning frame calls for assessment of stability and support, not a coating-only quote. A beam crack near a joint calls for the connection and its role to be checked. These are possible enquiry scenarios, not a diagnosis of your structure. Avoid using a visibly unstable area and describe the change when arranging the visit.

Timber protection and finishing

The agreed work can include protection and surface treatment associated with the repair. Replacement timber, cut ends and exposed faces need treatment compatible with the selected material and finish. Record whether preparation, primer, local paint or broader recoating is included. Weathered retained members may look different from replacement material; appearance and practical finish expectations should be discussed before ordering.

Repair is separate from rebuilding or changing use

This service does not automatically cover rebuilding the whole pergola, increasing spans, relocating posts, adding roofing or changing the structure's use. Those changes create a different project brief with their own design and approval considerations. Roof covering, flashing and waterproofing work are identified separately. For a new outdoor layout, use outdoor living carpentry rather than treating it as a minor repair variation.

A clear pergola repair quote

Identify each affected member, retained components, connections, access equipment, any temporary support, specialist responsibilities and finishing. Hidden damage found after opening a connection may change the scope. Agree the proposed additional work, price and timing before it proceeds. Existing photos and documents are helpful but optional for first contact; an on-site assessment establishes the accessible condition and next steps.

Timber Pergola Repairs: questions answered

Can just one rotten pergola post be replaced?

A single-post replacement may be suitable after the remaining timber, connections and supports are assessed. For a load-bearing member, confirm the design, temporary support, sequence and necessary approval arrangements before removal. A leaning structure should not be treated as a simple decorative replacement.

Why do pergola posts deteriorate close to the ground?

Persistent wetting, drainage, ground contact and exposed end grain can influence deterioration. The actual conditions need checking; new timber or a fresh coating does not by itself resolve a continuing moisture source.

Does pergola repair include a new roof or a larger structure?

No. Roof changes, increased spans, relocated posts and full rebuilding are separate briefs. The repair quote identifies retained and replaced timber and any roofing or specialist interfaces.
