SERVICE 10

Restoration & Maintenance

MEL ONE assesses accessible timber damage and discusses repair options for Adelaide homes. Trace the water or pest issue, identify sound material and agree any opening-up before defining the carpentry repair.

Termite damage repair

A licensed pest controller assesses and treats pest activity and advises on barriers. MEL ONE scopes damaged timber repair separately, with engineering advice where structural members are affected.

Moisture rot repair

Water-damaged timber repairs depend on the extent of sound material and the cause of wetting. Identify roofing, plumbing, waterproofing or ventilation work that needs the responsible specialist before closing repaired areas.

Structural assessment

Where damage affects a structural member or load path, obtain assessment and repair direction from the responsible engineer. A carpentry condition review does not replace structural design or certification.

Existing pergola posts and beams: define the damaged area

If an existing timber pergola shows soft timber, movement or decay near a post base or beam end, keep the affected area out of use where it appears unsafe and arrange assessment. Describe the location, visible damage and any roof covering without climbing or opening it up yourself. The assessment must establish the structural role, extent and responsible design or specialist input before any repair is accepted. Post or beam replacement, roof work, temporary support and adjoining finishes must be expressly scoped; a general timber-repair enquiry does not confirm all of those services.

Record timber damage without assuming the repair method

A surface mark, soft sill and damaged structural member can need very different responses. An optional wide photo shows the element in its setting; an optional close view can show accessible damage. Existing pest reports, moisture findings or project advice help identify the next assessment. We record the accessible condition and proposed carpentry scope, with concealed areas and further investigation clearly separated from the agreed work.

Diagnose the cause before repairing the symptom

Rot, movement, staining, jammed doors and soft timber can point to more than one cause. We start by observing the affected area, moisture paths, ventilation, nearby services and the extent that can be seen safely. Repairing only the visible face without addressing the underlying water, pest or movement issue can create a short-lived result and unnecessary repeat cost.

Make the damaged area safe and accessible

MEL ONE checks the damaged timber and arranges a safe assessment of the affected area. We discuss any temporary protection, support or access work needed, identify the repair scope and confirm the quote. Where pest, electrical, plumbing, engineering or hazardous-material work is required, we confirm the qualified service arrangements before permanent work begins.

Repair, splice or replace

Not every damaged timber member needs complete replacement, and not every local patch is suitable. We discuss what is sound, what needs to be removed, how the repaired area will connect to existing work and what finishing repairs follow. The chosen method should suit the member's role, access and condition, with structural decisions referred to the appropriate project professional where necessary.

Coordinate moisture and pest prevention

Carpentry repair is often one part of a broader remedy. Drainage, flashing, plumbing leaks, ventilation, coatings and pest management may all affect the long-term outcome. We identify the interfaces that need attention and coordinate our scope with the owner, builder or specialist trades so a repaired timber element is not returned to the same damaging conditions.

Document condition and agreed scope

We inspect the affected timber and joints on site and explain the repair scope before work starts. If opening a concealed area is needed, we discuss and agree that work with you first. Photos and condition notes support clear decisions and handover records.

What to send for a repair assessment

Photos from near and far, the address, age or type of building, when the issue was first noticed, and any reports from pest, plumbing or building professionals are helpful. Mention access limitations and whether the area is occupied. MEL ONE uses this information to prepare the site assessment. We inspect the timber, investigate moisture or movement concerns and confirm the restoration scope and written quote, including any qualified investigation required.

Discuss the damaged timber — questions

Can rotten timber be repaired without replacing the whole member?

A local repair may be possible where sufficient sound timber remains and the function can be restored. Extent, access and structural role determine whether a splice, component replacement or wider assessment is appropriate.

Does termite-damage carpentry include pest treatment?

Pest inspection, treatment and barrier design belong with a licensed pest controller. Carpentry repair should be coordinated after the pest issue and structural implications have been assessed by the responsible specialists.

What needs checking before a rotten pergola post or beam is replaced?

Confirm the member’s structural role, accessible damage, source of wetting, roof or other loads and any required design advice. Agree whether the proposed timber repair is within the attending team’s scope, including access, support and specialist interfaces. Record concealed decay and obtain approval for any revised cost or sequence before extra work proceeds.
