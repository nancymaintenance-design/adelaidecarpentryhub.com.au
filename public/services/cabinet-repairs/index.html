Cabinet Door & Drawer Repairs

MEL ONE Carpentry repairs existing cupboard doors, drawers and associated cabinet timber in Adelaide. We check whether a dropped door or failing drawer runner is a hardware problem, a damaged fixing point or deterioration in the board before agreeing the repair.

Cupboard doors that drop, rub or will not close evenly

Uneven door gaps, rubbing edges, loose hinges and failed soft-close action can have different causes. We inspect the hinge, mounting plate, cabinet alignment and material receiving the fixing. Adjustment may suit a sound assembly; failed hardware may require a compatible replacement. The condition of the board is part of the assessment, not an assumption made because the hinge itself is new.

A pulled-out hinge may be a board problem

A local fixing point can sometimes be repaired when the remaining material is suitable. Widespread damage, swelling or a broken hinge mounting area may need board repair or replacement instead. We distinguish a cabinet hinge pulled out of sound material from one attached to water-damaged board. The written scope lists hardware and cabinet material work separately so the underlying fault is not hidden by an adjustment.

Drawer runners that jam or let the drawer fall off track

A drawer that tilts, jams or repeatedly comes off its runners needs the runner system and drawer box checked together. The inspection considers box squareness, fixing positions, runner condition and alignment of the drawer front. Work can include adjustment or replacement with suitable hardware and restoration of the front position. Available replacement parts and installation requirements affect the agreed scope.

Match replacement hardware to the existing assembly

Hinges and runners are not interchangeable solely because they look alike. The existing system, mounting arrangement, dimensions and intended function guide the selection of replacement components. Record whether the owner or MEL ONE supplies the parts and whether availability has been checked. Changes to the cabinet or drawer needed to accept a different system should be identified before the replacement is ordered.

Swollen cabinetry below a sink

A dropped cupboard door below a sink with swollen surrounding board needs more than an assumed hinge swap. Moisture damage and its source must be considered, with plumbing responsibilities kept separate from the carpentry repair. A drawer that falls off its track even without overloading is another scenario worth describing. These examples help explain an enquiry; they do not diagnose concealed damage without inspection.

Repairing one component rather than replacing all cabinetry

A fault in a door, drawer, hinge or runner does not automatically require an entirely new kitchen. Where the retained cabinet is suitable, local work can be scoped around the affected components. If the board or cabinet box needs replacement, that work is listed separately. New manufacture, changed layouts and complete cabinetry projects belong with the kitchen and bathroom cabinetry service.

Check operation after the agreed repair

The completion check covers opening and closing, alignment, drawer travel and applicable soft-close or locking functions. The check is specific to the hardware and repaired assembly. Unresolved damage outside that assembly is recorded rather than silently included. Confirm any finishing, colour matching, removal and making-good work in the quote instead of assuming a hardware repair covers the entire surrounding cabinet.

Preparing a cabinet repair enquiry

Tell MEL ONE which doors or drawers are affected, how they behave and whether the adjacent board is swollen, cracked or loose. An existing hardware label or safely taken photo can help identify the system but is optional for first contact. We arrange an assessment, confirm access and any assessment charge, then provide a written repair scope. Cabinet work and plumbing, electrical or other specialist interfaces remain separate.

Cabinet Door & Drawer Repairs: questions answered

Can damaged cabinet hinge holes be repaired?

Some local fixing areas can be repaired when sufficient suitable material remains. Swollen board or extensive damage around the hinge may require a separately scoped board repair or replacement; a new hinge alone may not resolve it.

Why does a kitchen drawer keep falling off its runners?

Runner damage, alignment, loose fixings or the drawer box can contribute. MEL ONE checks the assembly and compatible parts before agreeing adjustment or runner replacement rather than assuming overloading is the only cause.

Does MEL ONE Carpentry handle a small cupboard repair?

Yes. MEL ONE Carpentry assesses cupboard door, drawer, hardware and associated cabinet material repairs. A changed kitchen layout or new cabinetry is a different project brief.
