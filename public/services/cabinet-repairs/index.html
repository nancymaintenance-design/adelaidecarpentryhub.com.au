Cabinet Hinges Pulled Out: Hardware and Damaged Boards

A cupboard door that drops, rubs or closes unevenly can have loose hardware, a damaged mounting area or an alignment fault. MEL ONE Carpentry checks the hinge, mounting plate, cabinet and the board receiving the fixing. Adjustment may suit a sound assembly, but a new hinge alone will not repair unsuitable material behind it.

Some local hinge fixing areas can be repaired where enough suitable board remains. Swelling, splitting or extensive damage may require separately scoped board repair or replacement. A dropped door beneath a sink also needs the moisture source considered, with plumbing responsibilities kept distinct from cabinet carpentry.

Drawer Runners Failing or Repeatedly Leaving the Track

A drawer that jams, tilts or falls off its runners needs the runner system and drawer box checked together. The fault can involve alignment, loose fixings, damaged hardware or an out-of-square box, even when the drawer is not overloaded. Inspect the front position and mounting arrangement before choosing adjustment or replacement.

Compatible replacement hardware depends on the existing system, dimensions, fixing positions and intended function. Similar appearance does not establish that a runner or soft-close mechanism will fit. Confirm parts availability and whether the drawer or cabinet needs modification to accept a different system before ordering.

Hinge Fixings, Drawer Runners and Small Repair Questions

Can damaged cabinet hinge holes be repaired?

Some local fixing areas can be repaired when sufficient suitable material remains. Swollen board or extensive damage around the hinge may require a separately scoped board repair or replacement; a new hinge alone may not resolve it.

Why does a kitchen drawer keep falling off its runners?

Runner damage, alignment, loose fixings or the drawer box can contribute. MEL ONE checks the assembly and compatible parts before agreeing adjustment or runner replacement rather than assuming overloading is the only cause.
