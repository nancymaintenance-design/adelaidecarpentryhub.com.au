SERVICE 03

Fix-Out & Second Fix

MEL ONE plans second-fix carpentry around room readiness, floor levels and door schedules. Define the finishing package early so trims, hardware and adjoining trades can meet with fewer unresolved junctions.

Cornices & mouldings

Timber mouldings and curtain-box details can form part of the finishing package. Identify plaster features, preparation and painting as separate trade interfaces where required.

Skirtings & architraves

Skirtings and architraves are selected around profiles, room conditions and adjoining finishes. Joint and fixing methods depend on material, wall condition and the agreed finish.

Door frames & leaves

Ordinary internal door installation includes the agreed frame, leaf and hardware adjustments. Fire-rated, acoustic, security and accessibility systems require specified products and appropriate specialist responsibilities.

Existing timber stairs: distinguish wear from a safety issue

A marked tread, loose trim and moving stair or handrail are not the same repair. Record the affected part and visible symptoms without dismantling it. The assessment needs to distinguish finish work from a structural or fall-protection concern before a task is accepted. Supports, changed dimensions, barriers and handrail performance need the responsible design and specialist review; a second-fix package does not automatically include every stair repair.

Timber stair installation

Treads, risers, balusters and handrails fitted and tuned on site for consistent step height and a straight rail.

The right time for second-fix work

Second fix performs best when framing, linings, floor levels and service rough-ins are sufficiently complete and dry. Starting too early can lead to damaged trim, changed openings or repeated work. We coordinate with the builder or site manager about readiness, delivery timing, room-by-room access and protection of finished surfaces.

Consistent reveals and junctions

The small lines around doors, windows, skirtings and built-ins are what make a room feel intentional. We measure from established reference points, account for floor finishes and check how trims meet at corners, stairs and cabinetry. This approach helps avoid visibly uneven reveals and awkward transitions that are expensive to correct after painting.

Door operation and hardware coordination

A door can look correct and still perform poorly if clearances, hinges, seals, locks or strike plates are not coordinated. We check handing, swing, required hardware, finished floor level and adjacent cabinetry before final adjustment. Fire, acoustic, security or accessibility requirements should be identified in the approved project documentation before installation.

Working with paint and flooring trades

Second-fix carpentry sits between several finishing trades. We plan sequencing with plaster, painting, flooring, glazing and cabinetry so joints are prepared appropriately and completed items are not damaged by later work. Straightforward communication about who owns caulking, filling, coating and touch-up prevents gaps in the final handover scope.

Finish review before handover

Before an area is signed off, we review alignment, mitres, fixing points, movement, hardware operation and visible damage in normal viewing conditions. Any agreed defects are easier to manage while materials and access are still available. This trade review complements, rather than replaces, the builder's quality process and any required certification.

Information that improves a second-fix quote

Describe the rooms, finish level, floor finishes and timing. Existing schedules, elevations, profiles and hardware information are optional for initial enquiry; confirm them before installation. Renovations also require checking existing openings and junctions before a detailed finishing package is priced.

Scope the finishing package — questions

When should second-fix carpentry be booked?

Confirm that linings, moisture conditions, openings and final floor levels are sufficiently settled. Agree room access and protection with the builder before deliveries and installation are scheduled.

Do I need a full second-fix package for one damaged skirting?

A local skirting or architrave repair can be scoped separately. Use the skirting-board or door-jamb service when the task is a specific trim problem rather than a coordinated finishing stage.

Does second fix include painting or specialist doors?

Filling, caulking, painting and specialist fire, acoustic or security door systems need explicit scope and responsible trades. Ordinary door carpentry does not establish a rated system's performance. Existing stair or handrail movement also needs assessment of its safety role; do not treat it as decorative trim without confirming the responsible scope.
