Floorboards Moving Underfoot: Dips, Bounce and Squeaks

A squeak, springy patch or dip near an internal wall is an observation rather than a diagnosis. Floorboards, their fixings, joists, bearers and connections can create different symptoms in the same area. MEL ONE checks the safely accessible condition before promising a particular repair or assuming the joists need replacement.

Note the room and location, whether the movement has changed and any known moisture or pest findings. Persistent bounce, visible deterioration or a moving support can require further professional assessment. A photograph or floor sound alone does not establish the structural cause or a finished floor-level outcome.

Subfloor Inspection Access and What Can Be Seen

Inspection depends on safe access to the relevant underside and supporting timber. Explain known access openings, restricted spaces and moisture or other conditions beneath the floor. Do not enter an unsafe subfloor area to produce enquiry photos; access limits should be discussed as part of the visit.

MEL ONE examines accessible bearers, joists, support ends, joints and connections relevant to the affected floor. Visible moisture, decay, deformation and movement help define the next assessment. Concealed or unreachable parts remain recorded limitations rather than being presented as inspected or sound.

Local Joist, Bearer and Connection Repairs

An agreed local repair can involve approved strengthening, replacement of affected timber or connection updates. The method follows the confirmed condition and design requirements, with retained members and new work identified in the scope. A board-fixing problem is not automatically the same job as deteriorated supporting timber.

Load-bearing work needs the required professional assessment, confirmed design and planned temporary-support sequence before alteration. MEL ONE's carpentry scope states the approved repair and information it relies on. If exposing the member changes the finding, agree the revised method, responsibilities and price before extending work.

Floor Access, Reinstatement and Moisture or Pest Interfaces

Some repairs need an agreed opening through the floor where underside access is insufficient. Define the boards removed, opening location, protection and carpentry reinstatement before the work is booked. Matching boards and any wider sanding, coating or adjoining trim repair should have explicit allowances.

Coordinate the repair with occupied rooms and other trades so the floor is not closed over unresolved work. Plumbing leaks, drainage, ventilation or pest treatment can need separate responsible providers. Replacing damaged timber does not establish that the moisture or pest cause has been resolved.

Local Subfloor Repair Quote and Wider-project Limits

A subfloor quote should identify members or connections, access, investigation limits, temporary support, approved repair work and reinstatement. Local repair does not include whole-house lifting, full floor levelling, restumping, foundation strengthening or structural redesign. If the evidence points to a broader condition, resolve that next assessment before treating a local timber repair as the remedy.

Restricted underside access, opening the floor and reinstating boards can change the allowance for the same member repair. Compare the approved method, support arrangements and reinstatement together. Keep the completion record and inspection limits with outstanding moisture, pest or structural follow-up.

Floor Movement, Supporting Timber and Levelling Questions

Does a squeaky or bouncing floor mean the joists need replacing?

No single symptom establishes that conclusion. Floorboards, fixings, supporting members and connections can produce different movement or noise problems. MEL ONE inspects the safely accessible area to distinguish those possibilities and defines any further professional assessment before confirming local strengthening, replacement or a connection repair.

Can MEL ONE repair load-bearing subfloor timber?

MEL ONE carries out approved local subfloor timber repairs, including work to supporting members where the required design and professional assessment are confirmed. Load-bearing work also needs a planned temporary-support sequence before alteration begins. The written scope identifies the approved repair, access, responsibilities and any floor reinstatement.

Will a local subfloor repair level the whole house?

A local repair is scoped to the assessed timber or connection problem and does not promise whole-house levelling. House lifting, full levelling, restumping, foundation strengthening and structural redesign are separate projects. If the inspection indicates a wider condition, the relevant assessment and next steps need to be resolved before choosing a repair.
