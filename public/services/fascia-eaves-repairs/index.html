Fascia, Bargeboard & Timber Eaves Repairs

MEL ONE provides fascia and timber eaves repairs in Adelaide, including bargeboards and local board replacement. We inspect visible timber damage and the adjoining fixing base, then agree the repair extent, access arrangements and any roof or gutter work that needs separate coordination.

Identify the board and the damage

Fascia, bargeboards and timber eaves occupy different parts of the roof edge, so an enquiry is more useful when it identifies the affected position. Tell MEL ONE whether damage runs along the gutter line, follows a sloping roof edge or appears beneath the eaves. We inspect rot, cracks, warping, open joints, peeling coatings and loose boards alongside the adjoining fixing base. Those observations help distinguish a local timber repair from a wider problem at the roof junction.

Look beyond peeling paint

A peeling finish can hide damaged timber, while a sound-looking board can have an unreliable connection behind it. We check the accessible board edges, ends, joints and fixing positions before confirming what can remain. A loose corner or opening joint needs attention to its connection as well as its appearance. Painting alone is not treated as evidence that the underlying board and fixing base are suitable to retain.

Choose a local repair, section or full board

The repair extent follows the condition found on site rather than the length of the most obvious stain. MEL ONE can carry out local repairs, replace a damaged section or replace a full board where that is the agreed approach. The decision considers remaining sound timber, the position of joints and whether the replacement can be fixed properly. The quote identifies the affected runs and explains where a larger replacement would change the work or finish.

Confirm the fixing base before replacement

Removing a damaged board may reveal deteriorated fixings or a damaged fixing batten that could not be assessed from the outside. We remove the agreed damaged boards and fixings, then check the exposed base before fitting replacement timber. Repairs to confirmed non-load-bearing fixing battens can form part of the carpentry scope. Rotten rafter tails or other roof-structure damage need a separate assessment and scope with the relevant qualified professionals.

Match thickness, width and visible profile

A replacement board should be considered in relation to the adjoining boards, rather than selected only by its face width. MEL ONE checks thickness, width and profile so the repaired run meets existing edges and corners coherently. Corners, joints and exposed ends are included in the discussion because small mismatches can interrupt an otherwise straight roof edge. Where an exact existing profile is unavailable, we explain the proposed match before the replacement is agreed.

Specify end protection and painting

Carpentry replacement and coating work are separate items to define in the quote. Discuss timber-end protection, primer, spot painting and a full recoat individually so the handover condition is clear. A local coating allowance does not automatically include repainting the entire fascia or eaves run. Tell us if a painter is already booked, because the timber repair sequence and agreed surface preparation need to fit that work.

Coordinate gutters, flashing and roof work

A damaged fascia can sit alongside a gutter or flashing issue without both tasks belonging to the same repair allowance. MEL ONE identifies those interfaces and agrees who will assess, remove, reinstate or repair the adjoining components. Gutter work, flashing work and roof-structure repairs are separately scoped and coordinated with qualified trades where needed. If opening the area reveals additional deterioration, the affected work is reviewed before extending the original timber repair.

Plan access and a clear handover

Roof-edge repairs need site access planning before the board quantities alone can produce a useful quote. Tell us about side passages, gardens, extensions and any areas beneath the affected eaves that are difficult to reach. We assess access on site and set out the removal, replacement, adjoining-trade and coating responsibilities in writing. At handover, the agreed repair and any remaining separately scoped work should be clear, including whether the timber is primed, locally painted or awaiting a full recoat.

Fascia and timber eaves repair questions

Does peeling fascia paint mean the whole board needs replacing?

Peeling paint alone does not establish the repair extent. MEL ONE checks timber condition, board ends, joints, fixings and the adjoining base before comparing local repair, section replacement and full-board replacement. Painting and timber replacement are specified separately, so the quote explains both the repair and the intended finish.

What happens if damaged timber is found behind the fascia?

The exposed fixing base is assessed before a replacement board is fitted. Repair of a confirmed non-load-bearing fixing batten can be included in the carpentry scope. Deteriorated rafter tails or roof structure need separate professional assessment and an agreed structural scope; they are not assumed to be covered by a fascia-board quote.

Are gutter repairs and a full repaint included?

Only when expressly included in the written scope. Gutters and flashing require their own work arrangements, while timber-end protection, primer, spot painting and a full recoat are separately quoted options. Confirming these items before booking helps coordinate the carpenter, painter and any relevant roof or gutter trade. Unknown old eaves linings need identification before disturbance; do not cut, drill or sand suspected asbestos materials. Asbestos removal is outside this timber repair service.
