Carpentry & Joinery

Choose the timber task first: repair an existing door, window, floor or trim, plan new joinery, or coordinate renovation carpentry. For a small job or several repairs, send a short list and your Adelaide location. MEL ONE checks the scope, access and assessment arrangements before confirming the next step.

Find the right starting point

For an existing fault, start with timber door and window repairs or timber damage and maintenance. A damaged frame or architrave has its own door jamb and interior trim scope. Describe the symptom and what has changed before choosing repair or replacement.

For new work, compare built-in storage, kitchen and bathroom cabinetry and new decks and outdoor timber. Existing deck repairs belong with deck restoration. For several rooms or staged work, renovation carpentry helps frame the package and trade interfaces.

Include the job, suburb, access and preferred timing in your initial project enquiry. Photos, dimensions and drawings are optional at this stage; email existing files if they help explain the work. Confirm assessment arrangements, any charges and what the written quote will cover before booking.
