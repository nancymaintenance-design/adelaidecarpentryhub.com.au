Site Measure, Written Quote and Carpentry Handover

The visit checks the timber and dimensions. The quote identifies the work and materials; handover records completed items, adjustments and care.

Inspect or measure the timber

Tell us the job, suburb and preferred timing. We discuss the next assessment or measure and confirm any visit, design or investigation charges before booking. Existing photos or drawings are optional.

Price the materials and work

After checking the accessible condition or measuring for new work, we identify the materials, installation tasks, exclusions and decisions still open. The written quote records the agreed scope and any specialist or approval responsibilities.

Repair, make and install

Confirm the selected materials, access and installation sequence before work begins. The agreed scope identifies who handles adjoining trades, technical documents and any required inspections.

Check operation and finishes

Review the agreed work, operation, finishes and outstanding items together. Keep the written scope, product information and any applicable warranty terms with the care instructions.
