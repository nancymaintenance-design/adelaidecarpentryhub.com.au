Timber Door Adjustments and Latch Repairs

A door scraping after new flooring may need a clearance adjustment, but hinges, leaf condition and the frame also need checking. Note where it catches, whether the latch still meets and whether the problem varies with weather. MEL ONE assesses the whole accessible opening before deciding whether adjustment, hardware work or local timber repair is suitable.

Replacement becomes a different decision when the leaf is extensively damaged or the opening itself needs work. If hinge screws repeatedly pull out of split or deteriorated jamb timber, repair the fixing base as part of the agreed scope rather than assuming another adjustment will hold. The focused door-frame service covers jamb and architrave repair where that is the main task.

Timber Window Frame and Sill Repairs

A soft sill, rotten lower frame, open joint and jammed sash are different faults even when they affect the same window. Inspect accessible timber, fixings and the opening mechanism before comparing retention, a local splice and component replacement. Enough suitable retained material and a workable connection are needed for a local repair.

Check water paths, coating failure, glazing interfaces and adjoining cladding so the repair does not ignore why the timber deteriorated. Glazing, waterproofing and major frame distortion need their own assessment and responsibilities. Period windows may also require recorded profiles and relevant heritage advice before damaged material is removed.

Sash Window Cords and Balance Repairs

Traditional sash cords connect the window to counterweights through pulleys; spring or spiral balances use a different support mechanism. Identify the existing system before parts are selected. A heavy sash that drops or is unstable should not be forced or repeatedly tested while an assessment is arranged.

For cord-and-weight work, the agreed scope can include bead or access-cover removal, cord replacement and reconnection, pulley checks and adjustment of the sash and sliding clearance. Replacement balances must match the sash weight, mechanism dimensions and installation arrangement, then be fitted and tested to the product requirements. After repair, check smooth travel, operation on both sides, stable stopping, latch and closing positions; broken glazing, major distortion and widespread rot remain separate findings.

Cavity Sliding Door Mechanisms and Access

A timber cavity slider can rub or fail to latch because of the leaf, rollers, track, guides or surrounding opening. Describe whether the fault occurs at one point or throughout travel and whether flooring or finishes have changed. The assessment needs to establish what can be accessed without disturbing the wall before adjustment or parts replacement is confirmed.

Concealed access, discontinued hardware and making-good can change the repair scope. French or external timber doors also need their meeting edges, hinges, thresholds and exposure considered. Automatic doors, garage doors, mainly aluminium or glass systems and specialist security or rated-door requirements need separately confirmed providers and scope.

Before quoting, we discuss which glazing, coating, security work, temporary protection, concealed access and wall or trim making-good are included or separately arranged. Door Mechanisms, Sash Balances and Frame Repair Questions

What should I record about a sticking cavity sliding door?

Note where it catches, whether the door has dropped and whether flooring or finishes changed. A site assessment checks the accessible leaf, guides, rollers and track before a repair is agreed. Concealed access and making-good must be included explicitly; do not force a jammed or unstable door.

What is the difference between sash cords and spiral balances?

Sash cords connect a traditional window sash to counterweights through pulleys. Spring or spiral balances support a sash using a different mechanism. MEL ONE identifies the existing system and matches parts to the sash weight, dimensions and installation requirements; a similar-looking balance is not necessarily compatible.
