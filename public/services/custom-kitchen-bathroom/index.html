Custom Kitchen & Bathroom Cabinetry

MEL ONE plans kitchen cabinet installation, vanity joinery and laundry storage around the room, appliances and selected finishes. Confirm layout, hardware, manufacture and installation responsibilities before committing to the cabinetry scope.

Full kitchen cabinetry

Kitchen cabinets and islands are scoped around the measured layout, selected doors, hardware and lawful benchtop product. New engineered-stone benchtops, panels and slabs are prohibited; legacy work and exemptions have separate conditions. Confirm the applicable rules and specialist interfaces before ordering.

Vanity joinery and wet-area interfaces

Vanity substrates, edging and supports must suit the selected product and exposure. Cabinet joinery does not replace wet-area waterproofing; plumbing and waterproofing responsibilities are separately agreed.

Laundry units

Stacking washer/dryer cabinets, storage and fold-out benches maximising space, with plumbing coordination.

Existing cupboard doors and drawers: repair or replace?

A dropped cupboard door, pulled-out hinge or drawer that no longer runs smoothly does not automatically require new cabinetry. MEL ONE Carpentry checks the hardware, mounting area and drawer box before proposing adjustment, suitable parts or local material repair. Swollen board and extensive hinge-area damage are quoted separately from a hardware change. Use cabinet door and drawer repairs for the existing fault; use this cabinetry service for new manufacture, a changed layout or a wider installation brief.

Laundry cabinets and vanity joinery need a clear brief

List the appliances or basin to be accommodated, storage needs, door and drawer preferences, and what existing cabinetry remains. Confirm the product dimensions and required access before the layout is approved. A cabinet allowance should identify the carcass, doors, hardware, installation and making-good, while plumbing, electrical connections, waterproofing and benchtop responsibilities stay separately assigned.

Measure & install

Site measure, agreed drawings and selections precede manufacture and installation. Confirm who supplies each component and which specialist trades handle benchtops, plumbing, electrical work and finishing.

Begin with the way you cook and live

Good cabinetry is organised around real use: who cooks, what needs to be stored, where appliances sit, how doors open and how the room is cleaned. We discuss workflow, storage pain points, preferred materials and the visual relationship to adjoining rooms before locking in a layout. This makes the design more useful than a catalogue of cabinet sizes.

Site measure and existing-condition checks

Walls are not always square, floors can vary and renovation services may not be where old drawings suggest. A detailed measure records dimensions, out-of-plumb conditions, service positions, access and adjoining finishes. This information is used to plan fillers, scribing, appliance clearances and installation tolerances before workshop production begins.

Materials, hardware and moisture exposure

Door finish, cabinet board, edge treatment, benchtop interface and hardware all affect the way cabinetry performs. We explain options in terms of location and use, including areas exposed to steam, splashes or cleaning. Wet-area waterproofing, plumbing and electrical work remain coordinated with the appropriately qualified project trades and the approved design.

Design approval before manufacture

Final drawings or agreed selections should clearly show layout, finishes, handles, appliance information, benchtop responsibility and any special details. Confirming these decisions before manufacture is an important quality step: it reduces late changes, allows lead times to be managed and ensures the client, builder and trades are working from the same intent.

Installation and trade handover

Cabinets are installed in a planned sequence that considers floor finishes, wall readiness, stone templating, splashbacks and services. We protect completed work and coordinate practical handover points with other trades. Final adjustment includes checking door and drawer operation, visible alignment and the interfaces that can only be assessed once the room comes together.

Useful information for a cabinetry consultation

Start with storage requirements, appliance models and the project stage. Existing room dimensions, plans, photos and inspiration images are optional. For bathroom and laundry cabinetry, discuss the plumbing and wall finishes so the joinery scope and specialist interfaces can be defined.

Prepare your cabinetry brief — questions

Does cabinetry work include a complete bathroom renovation?

Cabinets and vanity joinery are the focus here. Waterproofing, plumbing, electrical work, tiling and other bathroom work need their own agreed scope and appropriately qualified trades.

Can new engineered-stone benchtops be included?

New engineered-stone benchtops, panels and slabs are prohibited under the applicable South Australian rules. Legacy work and exemptions have separate conditions. Confirm a lawful selected product and specialist responsibilities before ordering; alternative stone products may still present silica risks.

  • SafeWork SA: engineered-stone prohibition
Should I replace a cupboard when only a door or drawer is damaged?

Not automatically. First assess the cabinet body, affected door or drawer and available hardware. Local repair may be worth comparing with replacement, but the written scope must confirm whether that task is included. For new manufacture, approve the measured layout, door materials, hardware and appliance clearances before ordering.
