SERVICE 09

Custom Doors, Windows & Furniture

MEL ONE discusses new timber doors and one-off furniture around the setting, dimensions and daily use. Existing sticking doors or damaged windows should first be assessed as repair work before a replacement is specified.

External timber door requirements

Entry and side doors are selected around exposure, frame interfaces, hardware and documented requirements for the opening. Confirm product and system evidence for specified weather or security performance.

Custom timber furniture

Dining, boardroom, bar, desk and bedside pieces to your space and habits, in local Blackwood, Oak or Jarrah.

French & sliding doors

French and sliding timber doors need coordinated tracks, clearances, seals and hardware. Confirm acoustic, weather or security requirements against the selected complete system.

Match the piece to its setting

A front door, heritage window, dining table and commercial bar have different performance, visual and maintenance needs. We begin with location, use, exposure, style and the relationship to surrounding finishes. This helps determine whether a solid-timber, engineered, glazed or mixed-material solution is appropriate before time is spent refining a profile or finish.

Measure openings and interfaces carefully

Existing openings can conceal uneven walls, changes in floor level, old fixings and service conflicts. A site measure records the actual conditions, hardware requirements, weather exposure and installation access. For replacement work, we also discuss what adjoining trims, linings, render or paint may be affected so the scope is understood before manufacture or removal begins.

Timber, glass, hardware and finish selection

Material and hardware choices influence security, weathering, feel and long-term maintenance. We explain the practical role of species, profile, glazing interface, hinges, tracks, locks, seals and coating systems. Where performance or regulatory requirements apply, the product selection and project documentation need to be coordinated with the appropriate designer, supplier or certifier.

Furniture built for daily use

For a table, desk, bench or bar, dimensions should follow the people and tasks it will serve. We discuss seating, leg clearance, cable needs, edge profiles, movement of solid timber and the type of wear the surface will receive. These details create furniture that is comfortable and repairable, not simply a striking object on delivery day.

Workshop finish and installation care

Fine timber work benefits from controlled preparation, protection in transport and careful site handling. We plan the order of finishing, hardware fitting, delivery and installation so exposed faces and edges are not unnecessarily damaged. Once fitted, we check alignment, operation and visible junctions, then discuss any care requirements relevant to the selected finish.

A useful custom-piece enquiry

Tell MEL ONE the intended use, timber or finish preferences and target timing. Existing photos, approximate dimensions, sketches, drawings and inspiration images are optional for the first enquiry; email them to handymanfelix.au2026@outlook.com if available. For external doors and windows, describe the opening and orientation; for furniture, describe who will use it. We measure on site and confirm the design, materials and quote before manufacture.

Discuss a new timber piece — questions

Should a sticking timber door be replaced with a custom door?

Not automatically. Adjustment, local timber repair or hardware work may restore operation. Start with Door & Window Repairs when the aim is to repair an existing opening.

Will a custom external door be weatherproof or security-rated?

Exposure, frame, seals, hardware and the selected product or system determine performance. Agree documented requirements and evidence for the complete opening rather than assuming solid timber establishes a rating.

What details help price a bespoke table or door?

Describe the intended use, dimensions, timber or finish preferences and access. For doors, add the opening, handing, glazing and hardware requirements; for furniture, discuss supports, edge details and delivery.
