External Timber Doors: Repair, Replacement and the Existing Opening

Before replacing a front or side door, assess the existing leaf, frame, hinges and threshold to see what can be retained. Front door water damage may extend into adjoining timber or arise from an unresolved weather junction. A door that sticks can sometimes be adjusted or locally repaired; widespread damage or a changed design may justify a replacement brief.

For a custom door, the site measure needs the opening dimensions, wall and floor conditions, handing and installation access. Describe weather exposure, the desired appearance and any documented security or performance requirements. A solid timber leaf alone does not establish the performance of the complete opening.

French and Timber Sliding Doors: Clearances and Hardware

French doors that fail to meet evenly can involve the leaves, hinges, frame or floor relationship. Check the existing condition before choosing a new set solely to correct an alignment fault. Paired leaves need their meeting edges, opening sequence, hardware and seals coordinated with the measured opening.

Timber sliding doors need the track, rollers, guides, supporting arrangement and access for maintenance considered with the leaf. Concealed tracks and heavy components can change installation and making-good requirements. Glazing, security systems, automatic equipment and specialist rated-door requirements need their own responsible providers and confirmed scope.

Custom Timber Furniture: Dimensions for Daily Use

A table, desk, bench or bar needs dimensions that suit its users and setting, including seating, leg clearance and movement around it. Discuss what the piece will hold, how it will be cleaned and whether cables or equipment need accommodation. These choices shape the timber components and supports before the edge profile or finish is finalised.

Species, grain, construction and finish affect appearance and future care. Timber can vary naturally, so agree samples and matching expectations instead of assuming a reference photo defines every board. Delivery access, stairs and the route to the final position also need checking before a large piece is made.

Materials, Finishing and Installation Interfaces

For doors, coordinate timber, frame details, hinges, locks, seals, glazing interfaces and coating as one specification. For furniture, agree the visible surfaces, edges, supports and selected protective finish. The responsibilities for specialist hardware, glazing and adjoining paint or render repairs should be clear before manufacture.

Workshop preparation, protected transport and site fitting need a practical sequence. Review door alignment and operation or furniture placement and visible finish against the agreed detail at handover. Record the care requirements and any separately arranged finishing or specialist work that remains.

Bespoke Door and Furniture Quote Inclusions

A door quote should identify the leaf, frame or retained opening, hardware, glazing responsibilities, removal, installation and making-good. A furniture quote should describe the dimensions, material, construction, finish, delivery and any site fitting. Compare these items on the same specification before choosing between prices.

Door weight, hardware, exposure and retained-frame condition can change the installation allowance. For furniture, compare construction, supports and finish as well as dimensions. Keep approved details and care information for handover; existing door faults can first be assessed through the repair service.

Door Replacement, Performance and Furniture Quote Questions

Should a sticking timber door be replaced with a custom door?

Not automatically. Check the leaf, hinges, latch, floor clearance and frame to understand why it sticks. Adjustment, suitable hardware or local timber repair may restore operation where retained material is appropriate; extensive damage or a changed design can justify replacement. Start with Door & Window Repairs when the main aim is to repair an existing opening.

Will a custom external door be weatherproof or security-rated?

Exposure, frame, seals, hardware and the selected product or system determine performance. Agree documented requirements and evidence for the complete opening rather than assuming solid timber establishes a rating.

What details help price a bespoke table or door?

Describe the intended use, dimensions, timber or finish preferences and access. For doors, add the opening, handing, glazing and hardware requirements; for furniture, discuss supports, edge details and delivery.
