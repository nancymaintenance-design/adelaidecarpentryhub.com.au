How can I check who is authorised for my building work?

Before accepting a building-work quote, confirm the legal contracting entity, licence number, permitted work and responsible supervisor for that scope. Check the details through Consumer and Business Services and ask how specialist work will be contracted. The company identity and insurance details on our About page are separate records; they do not establish licence scope.

  • Check a licence holder with CBS
  • MEL ONE company identity and insurance information
Do you work with builders?

Builders can enquire about a defined carpentry or joinery package. Include the build stage, current drawings if available, supply responsibilities, access and proposed sequence. Before booking, agree the contracting party, scope, interfaces with other trades and who arranges inspections or design clarification.

What does custom cabinetry cost?

Cabinet dimensions, layout, door finish, internal fittings, hardware, removal and installation access affect the quote. Check whether benchtops, appliances, plumbing, electrical work and finishing are included or separately arranged. We measure for the agreed cabinetry scope and record inclusions, exclusions and selections in the written quote.

  • Kitchen and bathroom cabinetry scope
  • Understand cabinetry quote factors
How is decking priced?

A deck quote depends on whether the work is a new structure or repair, the accessible frame condition, area, height, timber and finish, access and removal needs. Engineering, approvals and adjoining drainage work may require separate arrangements. A site assessment helps define the work before material and labour allowances are agreed.

  • Existing deck restoration and repair
