Start with one specific need

Tell MEL ONE what needs attention or what you want built, and where the job is. We arrange an on-site assessment or measure, confirm any charges before booking and prepare a written quote for the agreed scope.

Help us prepare

  • The timber item and what needs to change: repair, local replacement or new work
  • Suburb or site location, number of items and any access or occupancy limits
  • A short list if several small carpentry jobs need attention; name who can authorise the work
  • Preferred timing, material or finish preferences, and a budget guide if useful
  • Existing drawings or reference photos and approximate dimensions are optional; email available files to handymanfelix.au2026@outlook.com
