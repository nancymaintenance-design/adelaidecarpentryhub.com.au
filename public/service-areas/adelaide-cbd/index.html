Timber Doors, Windows and Interior Joinery

A sticking door may need hinge or latch adjustment, local frame repair or a wider opening assessment. Describe where it catches and whether nearby trim has moved. For a timber window, identify the damaged sill, frame or moving part; the visit checks accessible timber, hardware and adjoining finishes before repair or replacement is specified.

Jambs, architraves and skirting can be considered with the opening they surround. For cabinetry, custom details or refurbishment, explain the intended use and which dimensions, finishes and other trades are already decided. Decks, gates and fences need separate checks of exposed timber, supports, moisture and access.

Building Entry, Loading and Approval to Work

Give the street, property type and room or outdoor area involved. Note occupied rooms, stairs or lifts, parking, loading and any actual building-manager requirements. Confirm who can authorise the visit and approve the work, particularly where access or timber belongs to a shared area.

Photos and approximate dimensions are optional. If useful, send a wide view and a close detail, then describe sticking, splitting, movement or dampness. The assessment establishes the accessible condition; the written quote should identify retained timber, replacement parts, concealed conditions and any separately arranged trade work.

For load-bearing damage, heritage controls or specialist trade work, agree the assessment, responsible professional and required documents before the affected repair proceeds.

What should I send for a timber window repair in Adelaide CBD?

The street, symptom and access arrangements are enough to start. Photos and approximate sizes are optional; if available, include the frame and moving parts. The assessment then establishes the repair scope and any adjoining work.

Can you help with a timber door that no longer closes properly?

We inspect the door, frame, hinges, hardware and surrounding trim on site to identify why the door binds and confirm the adjustment or repair scope and quote.
