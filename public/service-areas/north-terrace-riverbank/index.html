Timber Openings, Joinery and Exposed Components

Identify the particular door, window, joinery item or exposed timber and the change you have noticed. Accessible condition, hardware and measurements are checked at the visit before a repair, replacement or another trade decision is recorded. Jambs, skirting and adjoining finishes can be considered with the affected opening.

For fine joinery, describe the intended use and finish expectations. Decks, fences, gates and weather-exposed window or door timber also need moisture, drainage and maintenance observations. A surface finish does not establish the condition of concealed timber or its supports.

Local Carpentry FAQs

Do you cover North Terrace and Festival Drive?

Yes. North Terrace, Festival Drive and the listed Riverbank streets are included. Choose the street below to send it through with your enquiry.

Why do access details matter before carpentry work is discussed?

Access can affect how a site is assessed and scheduled. Sharing entry, loading, stairs, lift, parking or timing information helps avoid assumptions.

Can I ask about timber exposed to rain or moisture?

Yes. Note where the timber is, what weathering or moisture signs are visible and whether the issue affects a window, door, deck, fence, gate or other element.

What should a building manager confirm before a Riverbank assessment?

Confirm the person authorising the visit, entry arrangements and any actual induction or loading requirements. Explain which timber work is requested and whether the work area stays occupied. Availability, assessment charges and the responsible parties are agreed before booking.
