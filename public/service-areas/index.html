Local Repairs, Site Access and a Written Scope

Use the directory to find carpentry near your street in North Adelaide or the listed city precincts. Selecting a street adds it to your enquiry. If your street is not listed, contact MEL ONE to discuss availability and the work.

For sticking openings, use door and window repairs and the timber window gallery. Existing decks need repair or replacement assessment; new structures have a separate outdoor carpentry scope. For older details, read heritage carpentry and the condition-record guide before deciding what to retain.

Include entry, stairs, shared passages, parking or loading limits, occupied rooms and who can authorise the work. For renovations or workplace fitouts, identify adjoining trades and confirmed dimensions. Photos and drawings are optional. Agree the visit, any assessment charges and specialist or approval responsibilities before booking; the written quote records the agreed timber work and concealed-condition limits.

Enquiries are welcome seven days from 09:00 to 21:00 Adelaide time. A listed street does not establish availability for a particular project.
