Choose your next step

Turn a timber question into a clearer brief

Start with the guide closest to the decision you need to make. The deck replacement guide explains what to record before comparing repair and replacement, while the kitchen renovation cost guide helps you separate cabinetry choices from the wider renovation. For built-in storage, the wardrobe planning guide covers layout, measurements and quote inclusions.

Choose the scope, quote or material question

Unsure which timber trade to appoint? Read carpenter, joiner or cabinet maker before allocating the handovers. The solid versus engineered flooring guide compares products and restoration conditions. Use the matching service for the next discussion: decking and timber flooring, kitchen cabinetry, or built-in storage. Photos, dimensions and drawings are optional for the first enquiry; a site assessment establishes the accessible condition and agreed scope.

Separate conservation principles from repair preparation

Read the heritage restoration principles to consider significance, retention and evidence. Then use the heritage timber repair scope guide to prepare an element record, access notes and repair questions. Existing project records show how work develops: compare the deck replacement sequence and the heritage verandah gallery. These examples support planning; they do not establish what your property needs.

Before enquiring, note access restrictions, occupied rooms, preferred timing and which trades are involved. For an operating workplace, read the commercial fitout guide and review fitout and refurbishment for staging considerations. Bring unresolved questions to the first discussion so the written quote can identify inclusions, exclusions and the next decisions.
